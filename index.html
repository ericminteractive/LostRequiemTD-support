Privacy Policy

Lost Requiem TD · Last updated: September 29, 2026

Short version: the whole game plays offline. To rank your battles on the leaderboards, it keeps an account for you on the game's server. The account is anonymous unless you choose Sign in with Apple, and even then the game asks Apple for no name and no email address. Your chosen handle, your places on the boards, and your ranked battles are public. There is no advertising, no third-party analytics, and no tracking.

Information stored on your device

Your campaign (your heroes, their gear, skills, and medals, the maps you have won, and your Crown Marks), your settings, your War Journal, and the replays of your battles are saved on your device. Battles waiting to be uploaded are kept there too until you are online.

Your account

The first time you open the game, you choose to play as a guest or to sign in with Apple.

  • As a guest, the game makes an anonymous account for you. It is tied to this install of the game, so deleting the game may lose it.
  • With Sign in with Apple, Apple gives the game an identifier that is unique to this developer. The game does not ask Apple for your name or your email address, so it never receives them. Your campaign then follows your account to another device.

What is sent to the game's server, and why

Every battle is played again on the server before it can rank, so nobody can post a score they did not earn. For that, the game sends:

  • Your campaign's log: each change to your campaign, such as a battle (its setup and every order you gave), a purchase, a new item, or a skill point spent. The server applies the same changes with the game's own rules and ranks only what checks out.
  • Battle seeds: each ranked battle plays on a seed the server issues.
  • An App Attest key: once per install, Apple's App Attest confirms the game is a genuine copy on a real device, and the key it makes signs your uploads. It identifies the install, not you.
  • Purchase records: the signed transaction information Apple provides (the product, the date, and a transaction number), so the server can confirm a purchase before crediting scrying orbs. If Apple refunds a purchase, Apple tells the server, and orbs from it are taken back.
  • Daily Challenges: your challenge results, your places on the day's board, and your Laurels.

With no internet connection, the game keeps playing, and nothing is sent until you are online again.

What other players can see

  • Your handle, which you choose. A filter keeps out offensive names.
  • Your places and scores on the leaderboards, and the item that ranks you on the Best Item board.
  • Your player card: your handle, your places, and your campaign's standing (such as the maps you have won and your heroes' levels).
  • The replays of your ranked battles, which other players can watch.

There is no chat and no messaging, so no player can send you anything.

What is never collected

Your real name, your email address, your contacts, your location, your photos, and your advertising identifier. Nothing the game sends can recognize you in any other app.

Reports and blocking

You can report a player or a replay from inside the game. A report sends the player or battle you reported, the reason you chose, and your account's identifier to the developer, who reads it and can hide or remove a player from the leaderboards. Blocking a player hides them from you.

Where it is stored

The game's server is run for the developer by Supabase, Inc. (the database, accounts, and server functions) and Fly.io, Inc. (the machine that plays battles again to check them), which process the data only for this purpose. The developer receives report notifications by email through Resend. Sign in with Apple, App Attest, and purchases are provided by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy.

How long it is kept

A battle that no longer holds a place on any board, and nothing else needs, is deleted after 30 days. Your account and what belongs to it stay until you delete the account.

Deleting your account

Use "Delete my leaderboards account" in Settings. It removes your account and everything under it from the server: your player profile, your battles and scores, the replays you unlocked, your scrying orbs, your blocks and reports, your App Attest keys, and your purchase records. If you signed in with Apple, the game also asks Apple to end its access. Your campaign on the device stays until you reset it or delete the game.

In-app purchases

Purchases are optional and are processed by Apple. The developer never receives your card number or your billing details, only the transaction information Apple provides to deliver and restore what you bought.

Advertising, analytics, and tracking

The game contains no advertising, no third-party analytics, and no tracking software. Nothing you do is tracked across other apps or websites, and no data is sold or shared with data brokers. Apple may provide the developer with anonymous crash and usage diagnostics through App Store Connect if you have chosen to share those with developers, under Apple's own terms.

Children's privacy

The game is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from them. Handles are filtered, and there is no chat or messaging.
