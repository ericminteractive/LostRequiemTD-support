Common questions

I found a bug, or I have feedback or an idea.
Please email me. Feedback shapes what I work on next.

Will my campaign carry over to a new device?
Yes, if you sign in with Apple (in Settings, or when you first open the game): your campaign follows your account. A guest's campaign lives on the device, so deleting the game may lose it.

A purchase didn't arrive.
Open the Treasury and tap "Restore purchases." If that doesn't bring it back, email me with your App Store receipt and I will make it right.

Why isn't my battle on the leaderboards yet?
Every ranked battle is played again on the game's server before it counts, so no one can post a score they didn't earn. That usually takes a minute or two once you're online. When it's approved, the victory's line in your War Journal takes a small seal. The game itself never waits on the server, so you can keep playing offline.

How do I report a player or a replay?
Open the player's card from a leaderboard, or pause a replay, and choose Report. Reports are read by a person.

How do I delete my account?
In Settings, choose "Delete my leaderboards account." It removes your account and everything under it from the server.
